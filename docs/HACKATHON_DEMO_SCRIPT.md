# 🏆 Uzhavan Thunai (உழவன் துணை) - 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script
**National Level Hackathon | Theme: Agriculture and Food Processing**

---

### [0:00 - 0:30] Hook & Problem Statement
*(Speaker holds up mobile phone running Uzhavan Thunai in Tamil mode)*
> "Respected judges, meet Muthuvel, a smallholder tomato and goat farmer in Dindigul, Tamil Nadu. Today morning he harvested 1,200 kg of fresh tomatoes. A local middleman arrived at his farm-gate offering just ₹28/kg. If he sells right now to that middleman, he earns ₹33,600.
> But what if he ships to Oddanchatram or Koyambedu? Will the high transport freight and mandi commission eat up his profits? What if prices crash tomorrow?
> **This information asymmetry robs Indian farmers of 35% of their hard-earned income.**
> Today, we present **Uzhavan Thunai (உழவன் துணை)**: India's first mobile-first AI advisor that empowers farmers to sell at the **RIGHT TIME, RIGHT PLACE, and RIGHT PRICE**!"

---

### [0:30 - 1:15] Hero Feature 1: The Smart Sell Advisor
*(Switch to screen view of the Smart Sell Advisor)*
> "Watch how simple this is. Muthuvel opens the app. He doesn't need to read English or type complex forms.
> He selects 'Tomato', inputs 1,200 kg, and his district Dindigul.
> Instantly, our **Smart Sell Advisor** calculates his **Net In-Hand Take-Home Profit** across every major market in Tamil Nadu—deducting distance-based diesel freight and mandi commissions.
> Look at the clear verdict:
> **'Hold 4 Days & Ship to Oddanchatram!'**
> Why? Because our LightGBM + LSTM Quantile forecast predicts rainfall in the Andhra border will delay arrivals, causing prices to spike by ₹6/kg.
> By holding and pooling transport with neighbor farmers through our FPO module, Muthuvel takes home **₹38,200**—pocketing **₹6,400 in EXTRA cash** compared to the village middleman!"

---

### [1:15 - 2:00] Hero Feature 2: AI Crop Doctor & Animal Doctor
*(Tap on AI Doctor and switch to Animal Doctor)*
> "Smallholder farming in Tamil Nadu isn't just crops—it's dairy cows, Salem Black goats, and country hens.
> What happens when an animal gets injured or produce starts rotting?
> Watch this:
> 1. **Crop Doctor**: Muthuvel snaps a photo of a tomato crate. Our edge **MobileNetV3 model runs on-device in 38 milliseconds**—grading it Grade 'A' or detecting *Erwinia* bacterial soft rot, warning him before the whole crate spoils!
> 2. **Animal Doctor**: His Salem Black goat has a puncture wound on its leg. He scans the wound. The AI identifies moderate laceration with fly-strike risk, and immediately provides **Traditional Tamil Nattu Vaithiyam** (Pure turmeric + cold-pressed neem oil + camphor paste) alongside veterinary antibiotics, plus a one-click call to the Dindigul Veterinary Hospital!"

---

### [2:00 - 2:35] Hero Feature 3: 10 Indian Languages & Multilingual Voice AI
*(Click Language Switcher to Telugu, then back to Tamil; tap the Voice AI mic)*
> "Judge the accessibility for low-literacy farmers:
> With one tap, the **entire app A-to-Z converts into 10 Indian languages**—Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, and English!
> And if he speaks in Tamil:
> *(Speak into app: 'தக்காளி விலை என்ன?')*
> The app speaks back in natural colloquial Tamil:
> *(App speaks: 'இன்று ஒட்டன்சத்திரத்தில் தக்காளி கிலோ ₹38... 3 நாட்கள் காத்திருந்து விற்கவும்!')*
> If you ask in Telugu, it replies in Telugu. If in Hindi, it replies in Hindi!"
