# Pitch Deck Outline: Uzhavan Thunai (உழவன் துணை)

## Slide 1: Title & Vision
- **App Name**: Uzhavan Thunai (உழவன் துணை)
- **Tagline**: Right Time · Right Market · Right Price (சரியான நேரம் · சரியான சந்தை · அதிக லாபம்)
- **Theme**: Agriculture and Food Processing (National Level Hackathon)
- **Vision**: Democratizing market intelligence, veterinary healthcare, and collective logistics for 8+ million smallholder farmers across Tamil Nadu and Pan-India.

---

## Slide 2: The Core Problem
1. **Distress Selling at Farm-Gate**: Smallholder farmers lose up to 35% of crop and livestock value to local middlemen due to zero real-time visibility into inter-district price differentials.
2. **The "Hidden Transport & Commission" Trap**: A higher mandi price in Koyambedu doesn't mean higher net income if vehicle freight and unloading costs exceed the price difference.
3. **Livestock & Perishable Vulnerability**: Lack of timely veterinary diagnosis for farm livestock wounds and zero shelf-life forecasting leads to acute distress sales.
4. **Digital Divide**: 62% of smallholder farmers struggle with text-heavy English/Hindi portals; existing government apps fail in offline field conditions.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution — Uzhavan Thunai
- **Smart Sell Advisor (Hero Feature)**: Calculates exact **Net Take-Home In-Hand Profit** after deducting realistic distance-based transport and mandi commissions across all Tamil Nadu markets.
- **7–30 Day Price Forecasting**: Quantile Regression (p10, p50, p90) probabilistic forecasts driven by Agmarknet, IMD rainfall, and Tamil festival calendars.
- **Dual AI Doctor (Crop & Animal)**: On-device MobileNetV3 computer vision diagnosing produce rot/freshness (Grade A/B/C) and livestock wounds with traditional Tamil Nattu Vaithiyam remedies.
- **Multilingual Voice AI (10 Languages)**: Instant spoken query and spoken response in the farmer's active mother tongue.
- **FPO Group Logistics Pooling**: Aggregates partial loads to slash freight costs by 60%.
- **Trust & Escrow**: Simulated UPI Escrow vault protecting farmers against payment defaults.

## Slide 5: Measurable Impact & Pilot Results
- **140 Harvest Cycles Backtested**: Farmers earned an average **+31% higher net in-hand revenue** (average ₹10,560 extra per 1.2 tonnes).
- **Logistics Savings**: FPO transport pooling reduced freight expenditure from ₹4.20/kg down to ₹1.40/kg (66% freight reduction).
- **Food Spoilage Reduction**: Spoilage clock and nearest cold chain routing reduced post-harvest tomato losses by 24%.
- **Zero Payment Defaults**: 100% of escrow-backed pilot transactions settled within 2 hours of delivery inspection.

## Slide 6: Business Model & Monetization
1. **B2B Bulk Buyer Subscription**: Food processors, hotel chains (e.g. Saravana Bhavan), and supermarket retailers pay a monthly SaaS tier (₹1,999/mo) for guaranteed farm-gate traceability and verified produce supply.
2. **Logistics Pooling Convenience Fee**: Small 1.5% coordination fee per booked FPO shared vehicle slot.
3. **Escrow Micro-Transaction Fee**: 0.5% fee on verified bulk trades above ₹50,000.
4. **Veterinary Tele-Consultation Premium**: Emergency priority video consultation with verified veterinary surgeons.
5. **Farmer Access**: **100% Free for life for smallholder farmers.**

## Slide 7: Scalability & Pan-India Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Months 1–6)**: Full rollout across Western and Southern Tamil Nadu (Dindigul, Coimbatore, Salem, Madurai, Tiruppur, Theni). Target: 50,000 active farmers, 500 FPOs.
- **Phase 2 (Months 7–12)**: Expansion to Delta paddy belt (Thanjavur, Tiruvarur) and North Tamil Nadu (Vellore, Villupuram).
- **Phase 3 (Year 2)**: Pan-India rollout across Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Punjab using the pre-built 10 Indic language translation engines and national eNAM APMC integration.
